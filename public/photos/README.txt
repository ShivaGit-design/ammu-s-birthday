Hey bro!
Place your real photos here in this folder (public/photos/).

For example:
- photo1.jpg  (The Beginning of Us)
- photo2.jpg  (Little Moments, Big Feelings)
- photo3.jpg  (Your Smile, My Favourite View)
- photo4.jpg  (More Memories to Come)

Then open `src/config.ts` and set:
imageUrl: '/photos/photo1.jpg' on the respective card!

(Or you can even click the "Change" button directly on the website while it's running to preview any photo instantly!)
